In this town, when the sun goes down, the demons come out. Every night. Right at seven. So everyone is home by six fifty nine. The baker closes early. The cop closes early. Even the mayor hides under his desk. The only ones outside at night are the Katana Guards. Long coats. Glowing swords. They never sleep. In other towns, kids want to be firefighters. Here? Every kid wants a katana. Me too. But I failed the guard test. Three times. I tripped over my own sword. Then one night, my little sister didn't come home. Six fifty eight. Then seven. The streets went dark. I grabbed a stick and ran outside. The demons were everywhere. Red eyes. Long claws. And in the middle of the square was my sister. Surrounded. I screamed, "Get away from her!" The demons turned to me. All of them. My knees were shaking. Then I saw it. A katana, lying on the ground. Glowing. I picked it up. I swung once. And I missed everything. The demons laughed. But my sister wasn't scared. She smiled. And her eyes turned red. Wait. My sister was a demon? She said, "Big brother, I was going to tell you." Then the Katana Guards arrived. Their captain raised his sword. I jumped in front of her. "She's my sister. She never hurt anyone." The captain stared at me for a long time. Then he lowered his sword. "Anyone who stands in front of a demon to protect her has the heart of a guard." That night, I finally got my katana. And my sister? She's the first demon on the team. She fights demons with us. And she's way better than me. Like and subscribe, or the demons will visit you at seven.

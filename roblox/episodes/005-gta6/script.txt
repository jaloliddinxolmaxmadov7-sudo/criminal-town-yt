In this town, there's one game everyone has been waiting for. For thirteen years. G T A Six. Babies were born waiting for it. Those babies are in high school now. Still waiting. My grandpa made a promise. "I'm not dying before G T A Six." He's ninety eight. He's still here. Out of spite. Every year, the game company says, "Next year." And every year, the whole town cries together in the square. Then one day, I walked past my neighbor's house. And through the window, I saw a kid. A small kid with a controller. On the screen: palm trees, fast cars, and the words, G T A Six. I couldn't breathe. The game wasn't out. Nobody had it. Not even the mayor. I knocked on the window. The kid looked at me and put a finger on his lips. "Shh." I said, "How?" He said, "Come back at midnight. Alone." At midnight, I came back. The kid was waiting. He said, "I'm not a kid. I'm from the year twenty forty." In twenty forty, G T A Six is old. Really old. He came back in time because in his time, everyone is waiting for G T A Seven. He said, "Wanna play?" I played for one minute. Best minute of my life. Then the door burst open. The whole town. Grandpa in front, running faster than I've ever seen. "Is that it? Is that G T A Six?" The kid panicked. He pressed a button on his watch. Flash. He was gone. And so was the game. Now the whole town thinks I'm a liar. Except grandpa. Grandpa looked at me and said, "How was it?" I said, "Grandpa, it was worth the wait." He smiled and said, "Then I'm waiting for Seven too." Like and subscribe, or you'll wait thirteen more years.

In this town, everyone becomes a Shinigami on their sixteenth birthday. A death god. You get a black robe, a sword, and one job: protect the souls of the town. The baker protects souls. The cop protects souls. Even Grandma protects souls, with a sword bigger than her. Because at night, the Hungry Ones come. Giant masked spirits that eat souls. The Shinigami stop them before anyone wakes up. On my sixteenth birthday, I waited for my robe. Nothing. No sword. No powers. I was the only person in town who couldn't even see the Hungry Ones. Mom baked me a cake. It said, "Better luck next year." Then one night, I woke up thirsty. And something huge was standing in our kitchen. A white mask. Long arms. Reaching for my mom. And I could see it. For the first time, I could see it. I grabbed a frying pan and hit it. It didn't even notice. Then a girl crashed through the window. A Shinigami from the next town. Short. Very angry. She fought the monster, and it threw her into the fridge. She looked at me. "You can see it? Then take my power." She pushed her glowing sword into my hands. My hoodie turned black. And the sword grew. And grew. Until it was the size of a door. I swung once. The Hungry One turned into light. Mom's soul floated back. The next morning, the whole town came to see my sword. The captain went pale. "Only one person ever had a sword this big. The first Shinigami of this town. He disappeared a hundred years ago." He showed me an old photo. The first Shinigami had my face. Same cap. Same chain. And the girl just smiled. "Took you long enough, Grandpa."

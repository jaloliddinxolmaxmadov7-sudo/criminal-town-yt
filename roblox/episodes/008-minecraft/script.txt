In this town, everyone plays Minecraft. Everyone. The mayor. The cop. Grandma has a diamond sword. But this town has one weird rule. Whatever you build in Minecraft appears in real life the next morning. Build a house, you wake up next to a real block house. Build a pool, boom, real pool. So the whole town is made of blocks. The baker built a cake factory. The cop built a jail out of obsidian. My neighbor built a castle and moved in. But there's a second rule. Whatever you destroy in the game disappears in real life too. So nobody uses TNT. Ever. TNT is illegal. Then my little brother got the tablet. He's six. At midnight I found him holding a block of TNT right next to the town hall. "Don't!" Too late. Click. The next morning, the town hall was gone. Just a giant hole. And the mayor was standing in it, in his pajamas. The whole town logged in to fix it. But my brother had built something else. A monster spawner. Under the school. That morning, zombies walked out of the school. Real ones. Square ones. The teachers screamed. The cop built a wall. The baker threw cakes. Nothing worked. Then I remembered. In Minecraft, zombies burn in the sun. But it was cloudy. So I opened the game, flew to the top of the sky, and broke every single cloud. Block by block. The sun came out. And the zombies turned into popcorn. The whole town cheered. The mayor gave me a medal made of real gold blocks. My brother got banned from the game for a year. But last night, I caught him on my laptop. Building something huge. With wings. "What is that?" He smiled. "A pet." Tomorrow is going to be a long day.
